# Sunday meal prep, three blocks (invented example transcript)

Welcome back. Sunday meal prep, the calm version. You do not need to cook five full meals. You need three building blocks.
Block one is a grain: cook a big pot of rice or barley, cool it fast on a tray, and it keeps four days in the fridge.
Block two is a roasted tray of vegetables. Cut them large, roast them hot, and they will taste good cold or warm.
Block three is a sauce. A yoghurt and lemon sauce or a tomato sauce turns the same blocks into different dinners.
Label the boxes with the day you cooked them. If you are unsure about a box on day five, do not eat it. That is the one rule that matters.
That is it. Sixty minutes on Sunday, and the whole week gets easier.
