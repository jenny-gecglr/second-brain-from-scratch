# Three pan weeknight dinners (invented example transcript)

Hello and welcome. Tonight we cook three dinners in three pans, and each one takes under thirty minutes.
First, the sheet pan chicken. Heat the oven to 220 degrees. Toss the thighs with oil, salt and paprika, and add potatoes cut small so they finish at the same time. Twenty five minutes, turn once.
Second, the skillet pasta. Put the pasta, the water and the tomatoes in one wide pan. No draining. Stir often and it is ready in twelve minutes, with a sauce that comes from the starch.
Third, the one pot rice. Fry an onion, add the rice, then stock at a ratio of one to two. Lid on, low heat, fifteen minutes, and do not lift the lid.
The big idea is simple: fewer pans, fewer dishes, and one habit, which is to prepare everything before you light the stove. Thanks for watching, and see you next week.
